# Almanca Öğren

A German course for a Turkish-speaking child who is starting from zero. All the words and sentences are written by hand, so the game is the same every time and needs no AI or internet connection.

## How to play

Double-click `index.html`. It opens in the browser; Chrome or Edge is best, because they usually include a German voice for the 🔊 buttons and the listening questions. It also works on a tablet if the folder is hosted somewhere, for example on GitHub Pages.

Progress (the stars) is saved in the browser on that device.

## What is in it

15 lessons, 210 words and 73 sentences:

1. Merhaba, Almanca! (greetings)
2. Sayı Avcısı (numbers 1 to 20)
3. Almanca Harfler ve Sesler (how German letters sound: sch, ei, ie, ä, ß ...)
4. Renk Avı (colours)
5. Hayvanlar Âlemi (animals; der / die / das starts here)
6. Benim Ailem (family)
7. Afiyet Olsun! (food and drink)
8. Vücudumuzu Tanıyalım (the body)
9. Ne Giysem? (clothes)
10. Okul Çantam (school)
11. Oyun Zamanı (toys and play)
12. Bugün Nasılsın? (feelings)
13. Günler ve Hava Durumu (days and weather)
14. Evimizi Gezelim (the house)
15. Ne Yapıyorum? (everyday verbs)

Every lesson runs the same way:

1. **Öğren**: new words in groups of five, each with a picture, its Turkish meaning and its sound. Articles are coloured: der blue, die red, das green.
2. **Alıştırma**: one easy question per word, straight after each group.
3. **Sınav**: 12 mixed questions (pick the German, pick the meaning, listen, der/die/das, spell with letter tiles, type it). Answers right first time earn up to three stars.
4. **Cümleler**: build short German sentences from word tiles.

A wrong answer is never punished: the right answer is shown and the question comes back a little later. Finishing a lesson unlocks the next one. After two lessons a **Karışık Tekrar** (mixed review) card appears. At the bottom of the home page, parents can unlock every lesson or reset the progress.

## Changing the content

Every word, meaning, sentence and tip is in `data.js`. The comment at the top of that file explains the format, so adding a word or a whole lesson needs no changes anywhere else.
